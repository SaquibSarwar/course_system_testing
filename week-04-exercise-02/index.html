Alive 2007

Album | 2007 | Daft Punk

Someone added it as this is their favorite Daft Punk album. The crowd's energy, the set arrangement, and the legendary musicality during their 2007 world tour make this a must-listen for any fan of French Electro. The sequence from "Television Rules the Nation / Crescendolls" building into "Too Long / Steam Machine" and then launching into "Around the World / Harder, Better, Faster, Stronger" is absolute perfection. Listening to this album fills me with energy, optimism, and inspiration. All neurons activated. Dance time is on. Please make music for us again, Robots!

Indiana Jones and the Great Circle

Game | 2024 | Action-adventure | MachineGames

This game was unexpectedly fun and a great way to enjoy the Indiana Jones universe from a different perspective. I had zero expectations and didn't play it immediately after release, but I'm glad I decided to buy it when it went on sale. Was it a perfect game? No! But was it an engaging single-player experience that let me pretend I was an adventurous archaeologist? Yes! And really,that's all you need from a game, right? **cracks whip emphatically!**
